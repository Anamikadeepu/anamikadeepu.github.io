Research paper · AI in critical care

XAutoNet: AI-Based Thermal Imaging for Early Sepsis Detection in ICU Patients

Author: Anamika · VIT Bhopal University

Sepsis causes an estimated 11 million deaths a year, and its early signs are easy to miss. This paper proposes XAutoNet, a convolutional neural network that reads thermal images of ICU patients, together with their EHR vitals, to predict sepsis up to 6 hours before onset. It watches for skin-temperature changes such as cooling in the hands and feet alongside a fever in the body's core.

  • Combines thermal-image patterns with clinical data (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation) for a fuller risk picture.
  • Uses Grad-CAM heatmaps so clinicians can see which regions drove each alert, so the model is not a black box.
  • Proposes a multi-centre prospective study with an embedded RCT, reported to CONSORT-AI standards, evaluated by AUC-ROC, PR-AUC, sensitivity and specificity.
